BEAVERBROOK · CREEKSIDE

A beaver’s view

Explore from just behind your beaver. Cross the water, inspect the pale root at the low bank, and find a branch in the grove. Bring it back to the family lodge by a route of your own.

Use WASD or arrows to move. Drag the view to look around. Press E nearby to interact. On touch, move with the left thumbstick and drag the open view to look.

Movement and discoveries save on this device. This is a separate movement experiment with a finite branch supply. Season and construction systems remain in First Reach.
