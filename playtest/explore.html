BEAVERBROOK · CREEKSIDE

Make a creek your own

Your family already has a lodge and a leaky dam. Willow grows ahead on this bank; larger trees stand across the creek and upstream. Harvest edible growth and wood, bring supplies home, improve the dam, or dig routes for easier hauling.

There is no required order or final checklist. Rest at the lodge to advance the day: the family eats, vegetation regrows, and flow changes. Food shortages let you recover and keep playing. The map marks your home and dam; discover the rest on foot.

Use WASD or arrows to move. Drag the view to orbit. The camera keeps a steady height; scenery fades when it blocks your beaver. Press E nearby to interact. On touch, move with the left thumbstick and drag the open view to look.
