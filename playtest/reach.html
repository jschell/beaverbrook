Work orders

Beavers gather, haul and build in this order. Unfinished work carries into the next season. Change priority or wood source at any time while paused.

    Run & rules

    The first dry season is season 3. Finish it in a sheltered occupied home with 4 stored food branches, or forage that can replenish a 2-branch harvest under current conditions. High water arrives in season 6; keep the chamber at least 0.10 above water. Every season eats 2. Keep playing after success. A shortage, exposed entrance or flooded chamber pauses the transition so you can recover.
