## Publication rule

Keep **In nature**, **what the evidence suggests**, and **In this game** distinct. Do not claim that every undammed stream needs restoration, every bank den is weaker, or every exposed lodge fails. The tutorial's authored failure is a scenario outcome, not a universal biological rule.
