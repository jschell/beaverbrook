# Prior art and transferable patterns

**Status:** synthesis of the source collection, not a new external gameplay audit. Links and mechanics should be rechecked before a detailed comparative study. No third-party assets or content were imported.

## Focused review of neighboring concepts

| Source concept | Useful pattern for Beaverbrook | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Crow & Co. | Observe → experiment → review; distinguish individual expertise from persistent player knowledge; cause-specific feedback | Do not import crow object inventory, social AI or genealogy |
| Acorn Empire | Immediate use versus seasonal reserves; resource choices reshape future vegetation | Do not import rival theft or caching chores |
| Shared animal-game collection | Small interacting systems, visible consequences, optional discovery journal, recoverable setbacks | Broader candidate list is context, not scope |
| Shared gameplay principles | Teach one rule safely then recombine; two viable approaches; previews, accessibility and voluntary replay | Reference patterns remain proposals until tested |

The [shared gameplay principles](gameplay-design-principles.md) also identify transferable patterns from board games and games such as Portal 2 and Into the Breach. For Beaverbrook, prioritize limited spatial choices, previews, visible objectives and combining known rules. These are design inspirations; no claim is made here about current versions or licenses.
