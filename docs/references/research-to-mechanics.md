## Rules for using research
1. Identify species and conditions; don't generalize New Caledonian crow tool studies to American crows.
2. Separate observed behavior from proposed explanation and playful invention.
3. Treat PBS/NOVA/NATURE as discovery paths, not reusable assets; verify claims against original studies.
4. Make mechanics fun without quizzes; optionally explain the real phenomenon after discovery.
5. For each proposed rule record: source, confidence, gameplay consequence, simplification, and a falsifiable playtest.
